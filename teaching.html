Teaching environmental health as evidence, systems, and practice.

My teaching experience spans environmental toxicology, air pollution, remote sensing, GIS, and introductory environmental health. I emphasize clear concepts, reproducible analysis, practical risk assessment, and the connection between environmental evidence and public-health decisions.
