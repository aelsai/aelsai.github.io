Mentoring

At Haramaya University, I advised undergraduate students and supported final-year research projects. My mentoring focuses on turning broad environmental-health questions into clear study designs, defensible datasets, transparent analyses, and readable scientific writing.

Community-based activities

I have led and contributed to community-based projects that raised awareness of environmental health, improved drinking-water quality, and assessed the health of vulnerable populations. At Haramaya University, I also participated in diversity initiatives aimed at strengthening inclusion and equity in education and community development.
